Systems

Modeling

Shape models without leaving the editor. Pick an object, move and add to its mesh with tools and shortcuts that work like Blender's, and save: the model updates in the scene by itself.

Opening it

Press Model in the toolbar. Scene View at the bottom of the panel, or Esc, goes back. It's there in 3D projects, not in 2D ones.

Select and Edit

The two buttons at the top of the panel are its two modes. Tab switches between them.

  • Select shows the whole scene. Pick an object with a model from the list or by clicking it, then press Edit. Right-click the view to add a shape to the scene as a new object.
  • Edit shows that object on its own, with its mesh drawn over it. Press Select to go back; if there are unsaved changes you're asked what to do with them.

Moving around

  • Right-drag turns the view around the selection, or around the model if nothing is selected. The scroll wheel zooms.
  • Numpad 1, 3 and 7 look from the front, right and top. Numpad . frames the selection. These are also on the right-click menu.

Selecting

Moving, turning and scaling

The handles move or scale the selection: Move and Scale in the panel choose which, and Ctrl snaps. Or press a key and move the mouse, with nothing to hold down:

Proportional editing (O) makes what's near the selection follow it, less the further away it is, for soft changes. Radius sets how far it reaches, and the scroll wheel changes it during a move. Position in the panel is where the selection is in the world, for exact values.

Adding shapes

In Edit mode, right-click the view and choose Add...: Plane, Cube, Circle, Sphere, Cylinder, Cone, Torus, Grid, Capsule or Crown. The shape becomes part of the model, at the selection or the middle of the model, and is left selected so you can move it.

Models are made of triangles in their files. When one is opened, pairs of triangles that form a flat four-sided face become quads, as they were in the program that made them, which is what edge loops and loop cuts follow. What you select is a corner of the mesh: files often store a corner several times, a cube has 24 vertices for its 8 corners, so every copy moves together and the surface doesn't tear. Lighting around a change is worked out again as you go.

Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes and Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes, for the last 64 changes.

Modifiers

A modifier changes how the model comes out without touching the mesh you're editing, so you can keep moving vertices and watch the result update. Press Add Modifier... in the panel. They run from the top of the list down, and order matters: Up and Down change it. The checkbox turns one off, X removes it, and Apply makes its result the mesh.

ModifierSettingsDoes
Subdivision SurfaceLevelsRounds the mesh by splitting every face. It comes out smooth-shaded.
ArrayCount, OffsetRepeats the mesh. Offset is the step between copies as a share of its size, so 1 on X puts them side by side.
BooleanOperation, ObjectDifference cuts another object out of this one, Union joins them, and Intersect keeps only where they overlap. Both should be closed solids. The other object stays where it is in the scene, and its faces take this model's first material.
DecimateRatioReduces the number of faces, keeping that share of triangles while staying as close to the shape as it can. The result is all triangles.
MirrorX, Y, ZAdds a reflection across the model's own center. Vertices on the mirror join up with their reflection, so model one half and get a joined whole.
SolidifyThickness, OffsetGives a surface thickness. Offset −1 grows it inward, 1 outward and 0 both ways.
SkinRadius, SidesWraps every edge in a tube, with a ball at every vertex, turning the mesh into a frame. Tubes and balls overlap at the joints rather than joining up.
ScrewAxis, Angle, Steps, HeightSweeps the mesh's open edges around an axis through the model's center, like a lathe. With a Height it climbs into a spiral. Model a profile off to one side of the axis.

Saving applies modifiers, because a model file has nowhere to keep them. Until you leave Edit you can still undo that. Changing a modifier's settings isn't an undo step, though applying one is.

Saving

Press Save Model, or Cmd/Ctrl+S. The scene editor sees the changed file and updates the model by itself, on every object that uses it.

The model isSaving
A .glb file, with vertices only movedChanges that file. Only the vertices change: its materials, textures and animations stay as they were.
A .glb file, with geometry added or removedWrites that file anew, with its meshes, materials and textures. Anything else in it, such as data from another program, isn't kept.
A built-in shape, or a .gltf, .obj or .fbx fileMakes a new .glb and this object uses it: built-in shapes go in Models/, others next to the original, which isn't changed. Other objects keep the old model. Save the scene afterwards so the object remembers its new model.

Saving replaces the file, and there's no undo after you leave Edit, so keep a copy if you may want the old shape back. Exporting a model again from another program replaces your edits. If another program changes a .glb while you're editing it, saving stops rather than overwriting that change.

Limits

  • Bevel, knife and sculpting aren't here yet.
  • Modifiers aren't kept in the model: opening it again shows the applied result.
  • A boolean handles up to 30,000 triangles between the two meshes. Slow modifiers update a few times a second during a drag.
  • Models with a skeleton can't be edited yet. Neither can one mesh placed several times in a file, or a .gltf or .fbx with animations, which a new file would lose.
  • A .glb with animations can have its vertices moved, but not added or removed, for the same reason.
  • A .glb with compressed vertices can't be saved. You're told when you press Edit.
  • New faces use the model's first material. Texture coordinates on them are simple, so a textured model may need them fixed in another program.
  • Past 60,000 vertices they aren't all drawn, though they can still be picked.
  • With X-Ray off, only faces turned toward you count. A face hidden behind another part of the model can still be picked.
